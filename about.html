About

What is Venture Lens?

Venture Lens is a curated research hub for anyone navigating the venture and technology landscape. It brings together hand-picked resources, deal flow intelligence, and startup directories — organized by industry vertical — so you can go deep on the spaces that matter to you, without the noise.

The hub covers five high-conviction verticals: AI & Deep Tech, FinTech & Web3, HealthTech & BioTech, Climate & Energy, and Wearables & Jewellery. Each vertical page is built around three layers of intelligence: curated tools and reading, notable recent funding rounds, and a directory of key companies.

Every entry is hand-selected. The goal isn't comprehensiveness — it's curation. Venture Lens is designed to be a starting point for research, not an exhaustive database. Think of it as a trusted reading list, updated as the landscape evolves.

Curator

If you know of a resource, company, or deal that belongs here, reach out via GitHub — contributions are always welcome.

Philosophy

Curation over aggregation

The internet has plenty of aggregators. What's rarer is a point of view. Venture Lens is opinionated by design — every entry represents a genuine recommendation, not an exhaustive list.

The verticals were chosen because they represent the most interesting intersections of capital, technology, and real-world impact in the current innovation cycle.

The resources were chosen because they're the ones actually worth reading — reports that cut through, newsletters with genuine insight, tools that practitioners actually use.
